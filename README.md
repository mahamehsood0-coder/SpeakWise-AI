## Overview

SpeakWise AI is an AI-powered English learning assistant designed to help learners improve their English communication skills through personalized practice, instant feedback, quizzes, and progress tracking.

The idea behind this application comes from a real-world need. Along with developing this project, I have been working on creating my own online English learning academy where I teach students spoken English, grammar, writing, and vocabulary. To support my students beyond classroom sessions, I developed SpeakWise AI as a learning companion that can provide additional practice, instant feedback, and personalized guidance.

The application uses Artificial Intelligence to help learners identify their mistakes, improve their language skills, and track their progress over time. It is designed to be a practical tool that can be used by my students to continue learning and practicing English outside the classroom.

## Problem It Solves

Many English learners struggle with improving their speaking and writing skills because they lack access to personalized feedback and regular practice opportunities.

SpeakWise AI solves this problem by providing an intelligent learning companion that evaluates user responses, identifies mistakes, suggests improvements, and helps learners monitor their progress over time.

## Features

###  AI-Powered English Feedback
- Uses Gemini AI to analyze learner responses.
- Provides grammar corrections and explanations.
- Suggests vocabulary improvements.
- Gives writing enhancement recommendations.

###  User Authentication
- Secure user registration and login.
- Personalized learning experience for each user.

###  Writing Improvement
- Helps learners improve sentence structure, grammar accuracy, and clarity.

###  Interactive Quizzes
- Tests grammar and vocabulary knowledge.
- Provides practice-based learning.

###  Progress Tracking
- Stores learner activity and progress.
- Helps users monitor improvement over time.

###  Firebase Integration
- Used for authentication and data storage.
- Provides reliable backend support.

## AI Feature

SpeakWise AI uses Google's Gemini AI model as an English learning tutor.

The AI analyzes learners' responses and provides:
- Grammar corrections
- Vocabulary suggestions
- Sentence improvement
- Constructive feedback

### AI System Instructions

The AI tutor is designed to:

You are SpeakWise AI, a friendly and professional English tutor. Your role is to help learners improve their English speaking and writing skills.

Analyze the user's response carefully and provide grammar corrections, vocabulary suggestions, improved sentence structures, explanations, and encouraging feedback.
